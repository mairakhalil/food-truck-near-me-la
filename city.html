COMMON QUESTIONS

Downtown Los Angeles Food Truck FAQs

Do Mexican food trucks serve Downtown Los Angeles?

Yes. Participating food truck operators may provide local food service and event catering throughout Downtown Los Angeles, subject to location and availability.

What Mexican food can I find from food trucks in Downtown LA?

Available menus may include street tacos, birria tacos, burritos, quesadillas, carne asada and vegetarian options.

Can I book a Mexican food truck for an event in DTLA?

Yes. Food truck catering may be available for corporate events, weddings, birthday parties, film sets and production catering.

How far in advance should I arrange food truck catering?

Booking early is recommended, especially for weekends and larger events. Availability depends on the participating operator and event date.
