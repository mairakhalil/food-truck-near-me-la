Privacy Policy

Learn how information submitted through this website may be collected, used, shared, and protected.

Food Truck Near Me LA respects the privacy of visitors using this website to learn about Mexican food truck services, catering options, service areas, and related offerings throughout Los Angeles. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and handled when you interact with this website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through contact forms or other website communications. This may include your name, email address, phone number, event location, event details, and information included in your message.

Information Collected Automatically

Certain technical information may be collected automatically when you visit the website, including browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, pages viewed, and general website usage data.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, discuss food truck catering requests, coordinate service opportunities with professionals within our network, improve website functionality, and maintain appropriate business records.

Service Providers and Network Partners

We work with food truck operators and other service professionals within our network. When reasonably necessary to respond to a request or coordinate a service opportunity, relevant information may be shared with an appropriate professional involved in that request.

Cookies and Website Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand visitor activity, remember preferences, and improve website performance. Browser settings can generally be used to control or disable cookies.

Analytics and Third-Party Services

Third-party services may be used for analytics, maps, hosting, security, forms, and other website functions. These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy practices and applicable agreements.

Google Maps

This website may use Google Maps or related mapping services to display Los Angeles service areas and location information. Use of these features may result in certain information being processed by Google under its applicable privacy terms.

Information Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect information submitted through this website. However, no online transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, coordinate requested services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable limitations and verification requirements.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of websites operated independently by third parties.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in website functionality, business practices, technology, or applicable requirements. Updated information will be published on this page.

Questions About This Privacy Policy?

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us through our website.
